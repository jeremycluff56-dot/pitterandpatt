Hi, we're Pitter & Pat

Printable party games & keepsakes for baby showers and bridal showers

Named for the pitter-pat of little feet, Pitter & Pat makes printable games and keepsakes for the two happiest parties there are: baby showers and bridal showers.

We believe hosting should feel like a party for the host, too. So everything we make is an instant download: you buy it, print it at home or at a print shop, and you're ready in minutes — no shipping, no waiting, no last-minute scramble. Answer keys come included, instructions are plain, and the designs are made to look beautiful in the photos you'll keep.

What we make

Baby Shower Games Bundle ($12) — 33 printable games, signs and activities in a modern sage green, gender-neutral design, from baby bingo and diaper raffle tickets to prediction cards and wishes for baby.

Bridal Shower Games Bundle ($14) — 35 games plus 5 signs in sage and terracotta, including He Said She Said, Would She Rather, bridal bingo and a mimosa bar sign.

Letters to Baby — Time Capsule Kit ($20) — our keepsake: guests write letters at the shower, seal them with milestone seals, and baby opens them on birthdays and big days all the way to eighteen.

Where to find us

Our shop lives on Etsy, our ideas live on Pinterest, and this little site is home base. Start with a free game if you're just browsing — we're glad you're here.
